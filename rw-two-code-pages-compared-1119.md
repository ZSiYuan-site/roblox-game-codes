# Lost Sword and Zenless Zone Zero codes: two very different routines

Two mobile games, two code pages, and almost nothing in common about how their coupons behave. Both pages are on the [Daily Reward Links](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/) tracker, and both were checked on November 19, 2026. This note puts them side by side so you can see what you are dealing with before you spend time pasting codes into the wrong menu.

## What is working right now

The [Lost Sword codes page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/lost-sword/codes/) lists four active codes: MORGANLINGERIE, YUMI, ASUKA and LSANNIVERSARY. The rewards are modest and partly unlabelled. YUMI gives 1,000 Diamonds, ASUKA gives ten character and ten pet summon tickets, and the other two are described only as free rewards because the two trackers that list them do not print the contents. The page keeps the description honest rather than filling in a plausible number.

The [Zenless Zone Zero codes page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/zenless-zone-zero/codes/) also lists four, but they are richer and stranger. ZENLESSGIFT is a permanent launch code worth 50 Polychrome, two Official Investigator Logs, three W-Engine Power Supplies and a Bangboo Algorithm Module. The other three, INKSHIFT33, LEVELUP33 and HOLLOWRUN33, each give 20 Polychrome and 2,222 Denny and are alternatives of one another, so only one will work per account.

## Where you redeem them

Lost Sword hides its coupon box behind the three-line menu in the top right, then a cogwheel, then the Account tab, then Redeem Coupon. Zenless Zone Zero asks you to reach Inter-Knot Level 5, finish the prologue, open the menu, tap More and choose Redemption Code; there is also an official web redemption page where you sign in and pick your server. Neither game accepts a code typed into chat or a profile field, and both are case sensitive.

## Why the dates matter more than the list

Lost Sword retires codes quickly. The official coupon posted on November 13, SPECIALNOVLTSD, had already expired by the time these pages were checked, and LTSDNOVEMBER comes from a single tracker, so neither appears in the working table. Zenless Zone Zero turns over even faster around livestreams: the version 3.3 stream code JRNY2SKY is already in the expired archive, and most of the old ZZZ2xx batch is gone too.

That is the real lesson from comparing the two pages. A long list of codes is not a better list if half of them stopped working weeks ago. Our rule is that a code needs two independently updated sources before it goes into the working table, and when sources disagree on the reward we write a generic description. The expired archive is kept for reference, not as padding.

If you only have a minute, start with ZENLESSGIFT, because it is permanent and has the biggest reward of the eight codes here. Then try the three Zenless alternatives one at a time. For Lost Sword, YUMI and ASUKA are the ones with firm, printed rewards.
