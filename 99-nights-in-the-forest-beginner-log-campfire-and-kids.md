# 99 Nights in the Forest beginner log: campfire levels and the four missing kids

99 Nights in the Forest, by Grandma's Favourite Games, asks you to survive 99 nights with friends: keep the campfire going, rescue four missing children and stay away from the deer that hunts at night. This log follows one 14 minute beginner video by Roodolf, with its numbers checked against our own pages. The video is from 2025, so class prices and item names may have changed since.

## Day 1: wood and the fire

Chop trees, keep the wood in your sack so you carry several at once, and replant every sapling you get. Feed the campfire until it reaches level 3. Then go looting for a spear, revolver or other weapon; the nicer a chest looks, the better the loot, according to the player. The video says the deer will not attack on your first day, so you can keep looting through the first night. Back at camp, put fuel in the fire and keep nuts, bolts and wood for crafting. The [99 Nights in the Forest beginner guide](https://newgamescout.com/99-nights-in-the-forest/guide) lists every step with timestamps.

## Day 2: the Pelt Trader

He arrives on day 2 and asks for a bunny foot, so attack bunnies until one drops it. He then offers three options. The player says to skip the old flashlight and take the good sack or the good axe.

## The first kid: Dino

Open the missing kids board, pick the Dino Kid and follow the arrow. Only attack the wolves with a red collar; they drop the red key. Headshots do more damage and save ammo. Open the door, loot the chest below, put the kid in your sack and carry him to the campfire. Each rescued kid adds extra days to every day cycle. Details for each kid are on the [99 Nights missing kids](https://newgamescout.com/99-nights-in-the-forest/missing-kids) page.

## Day 3: map, cultists, bed and sundial

Three wood crafts a map, opened with M. Gray areas are unexplored, so loot there. Cultists attack around day 3; kill all of them, because they drop a gem used to upgrade your crafting bench. Then craft an old bed and place it down, or you get no benefit: a bed adds extra days to every day. The sundial shows how much day or night is left.

## Kids two to four

The campfire level needed rises with each kid: level 4 for the Kraken Kid, 5 for the Squid Kid and 6 for the Koala Kid. The Kraken Kid is guarded by a gang of Alfa Wolves, so gear up first. A revolver is not enough for bears, so get a rifle and armor before the Squid Kid, and headshot the bears from a distance. For the Koala Kid the player estimates about 30 rifle shots and 12 revolver shots. Put raw food in the crockpot and eat only from it; press E to take the stew.

## After the fourth kid

Once all four are home you can idle, keeping fuel and food up. The player says that if your only goal is day 100, one more bed is all you need.

## Picking a first class

Classes cost between 10 and 500 diamonds. The video skips Camper and Medic and recommends the Scavenger for extra sack space, then the Cook at 40 diamonds. At 70 diamonds, Lumberjack suits melee and Ranger starts with a revolver and 18 ammo. Assassin at 500 diamonds is called the best.
