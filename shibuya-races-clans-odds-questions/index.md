# Shibuya races and clans odds: questions people keep asking

<p>Races and clans come up in nearly every Shibuya question, mostly because the creation screen prints a lot of numbers and little explanation. These are the questions that keep coming up, answered from the fan wiki's <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/races-and-clans">races and clans</a> page, which reads the odds off the creation screen in a player video.</p><h2>What are the odds for races?</h2><p>Death Painting is Mythic at 2%, Mutated Corpse is Epic at 4% (the shibuya.wiki fan site calls it Legendary), Incarnated Sorcerer is Rare at 7%, and Human and Cursed Spirit are Common at 87% together. Human trades a racial ability for an extra learned skill. Cursed Spirit regenerates with cursed energy, at a cost.</p><h2>What are the odds for clans?</h2><p>Itadori, Okkotsu and Zenin are Mythic at 0.25% each. Gojo, Hakari, Kamo, Kashimo and Tsukumo are Legendary at 1%. The Rare tier is 10%, and Sugawara is one of them with +5 health.</p><h2>Is there a pity system?</h2><p>The creation screen in the source video showed a line reading Legendary guaranteed in 89, under the Reroll button, along with a count of rolls left. That is what was seen on screen. The wiki has not tested the pity count itself, and the <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/reroll-guide">reroll guide</a> is the place to look for how to approach it.</p><h2>Which clan is best?</h2><p>The wiki's advice is to pick a clan for its bonus, not its rarity. Fan-site claims include that Okkotsu unlocks Rika with your cursed technique, Itadori's Black Flash no longer knocks back, Gojo has a 3% chance to awaken Six Eyes and Hakari has the biggest documented health bonus (+75). Those come from one fan site, so treat them as reported. There is a <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/clan-tier-list">clan tier list</a> if you want a ranking.</p><h2>Where do I spend reroll codes?</h2><p>In the Redeem Code box in the top right of the creation screen, before you lock in your character. Codes are announced on the Discord. The wiki keeps the <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/discord-and-trello">shibuya roblox trello</a> answer up to date: no official board is linked on Roblox, only the Discord. For the redeem steps, see <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/codes">Shibuya codes</a>.</p><h2>Can I change my race later?</h2><p>The wiki has not documented a way to change a race after creation, so assume you cannot. That is exactly why the beginner guide says to use your rerolls first.</p><h2>How reliable are these numbers?</h2><p>They come from a screen shown in one video, and the game is in Beta. The developers say things are subject to change. If your creation screen shows something different, trust your screen and tell the wiki through the <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/contact">contact page</a>.</p><p>More notes from the same set: <a href="https://shibuya-desert-yen-route-notes.a13715099660.workers.dev/">Shibuya desert run: yen, the gas mask and the gate, in order</a> and <a href="https://shibuya-roblox-trello-board-faq.a13715099660.workers.dev/">Shibuya Roblox Trello boards: a short FAQ on what is official</a>.</p>
