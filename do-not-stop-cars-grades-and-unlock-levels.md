# Do Not Stop cars: five grades, four unlock levels and one stat card

Do Not Stop is a Roblox driving game: you race a car to the shelter and the Runner never waits. The garage has a Cars tab, and unlike many new Roblox games it prints a plain grade letter on every car. This note lays out the five cars, what unlocks each one and where players are tempted to spend Robux. Everything is from the garage screens in player footage; we say where a stat card was not open. The [Do Not Stop cars page](https://donotstop.wiki/cars) has the table, and the [best cars in Do Not Stop](https://donotstop.wiki/best-cars) page has the reasoning.

## The five cars

| Car | Grade | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| Veltrano 4WD Turbo | D | Your starter. Stat card reads Speed 4, Accel 4, Grip 4, Brakes 4 |
| Hartmann 220 Sport | C | Level 4 |
| Rovelle Turbo 18 | B | Level 8, or inside the Starter Pack |
| Kanzaki | A | Level 13 |
| Branca | S | Level 20, the only S grade |

## What we rank by

We rank by the grade letter the game prints on each card, not by our own testing. Only the Veltrano's four bars were open in the footage we checked, so we cannot compare Speed or Grip numbers across cars. By the game's own grades the Branca is best. We have not seen its stats, so we cannot say how much better.

## Do not pay for the Hartmann

A Signal Lost screen offers the Hartmann as the next car for R$ 99, with +7% power and +5% brakes over the Veltrano. It unlocks at level 4 anyway, so the Robux buy only saves you a few runs.

## The Starter Pack

The shop's first tab is the Starter Pack: the Rovelle Turbo 18, a first drive skin and 2,500 credits. Level 8 is the free route to the same car. Whether the pack is worth it depends on how fast you level; a finished run showed +15 XP and +8 CR in one summary, so the climb is not instant. The [credits page](https://donotstop.wiki/credits) covers the codes RELEASE (250) and DONOTSTOP (200) and the VIP pass.

## Skins are separate

Skins and crates are cosmetic. The Works Crate holds 11 car skins at 62% Rare, 30% Epic and 8% Legendary, and nothing on the panels mentions speed or grip. Do not buy a crate hoping for a faster car.

## Practical order

1. Redeem the two confirmed codes on day one.
2. Finish runs and level; cars arrive at 4, 8, 13 and 20.
3. Try both handling modes with N before blaming the car.
