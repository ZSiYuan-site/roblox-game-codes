# 30 Floors Up bolt cutters: where they turned up and what we still do not know

Bolt cutters are one of those items in 30 Floors Up that everyone wonders about and few explain. Our page on [30 Floors Up bolt cutters](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/bolt-cutters) records exactly what two runs showed, and where the footage stops. This note is the same evidence in a slower reading.

## What the cards say

The item is a TOOL, not a weapon. The Basic 13 Tips video shows the Bolt Cutters card. We have not seen what they cut in the footage we checked, so we do not state a use.

## Where they turned up

The long co-op run picked them up on Day 4 and Day 5. Both runs were in the apartment while the objective was about checking supplies. We have not seen a fixed spawn, so we cannot say they always appear there.

## A hint from a chapter title

Sandoosky's video has a chapter called Searching for bolt cutters, between Escalating danger and Desperate final stand. At least one player had a reason to need them. The chapter title is all we have used from it, and we have not read the transcript.

## Inventory budget

You carry 4 items plus a backpack. A tool you do not know how to use takes a slot, so hold the tool slot free until you find the thing you need to cut. Our tip is to look around the apartment on Day 4 and Day 5, when the objective sends you to check supplies.

## How this fits the bigger plan

Supplies in this game are a budget: water, food, tools and noise. The [items and tools page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/items) lists the cards we have read, and the [ration planner](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/ration-planner) helps with food and water. Bolt cutters sit in the part of the list where evidence is thin.

## Why we publish thin evidence

A wiki that fills every gap with a guess ends up wrong. We would rather print two runs, a card and a chapter title, and say that is all. If you know what bolt cutters open in the current version, tell us through the report link and we will update the page with your source.

## What to check in your own run

If you find the bolt cutters, look at the prompt that appears when you face a locked or chained object, and note the Day. Two runs picked them up on Days 4 and 5, so a third run on Day 4 or 5 tells us whether the timing repeats. Those small observations are what turn a thin page into a useful one, and every correction we receive is credited to the video or run it came from.
