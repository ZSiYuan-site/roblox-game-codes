# Kitchen Knife, Rolling Pin or Baseball Bat: choosing a stopgap weapon in 30 Floors Up

You will not find the best weapon on Night 0. In [30 Floors Up Roblox](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up) the early apartments hand you whatever is on the shelves, and the question is which stopgap to carry until something better turns up. The item cards print Damage and Reach, so here is what those two numbers say about the cheap weapons.

### The cheap pair

The Kitchen Knife shows Damage 18 and Reach 4.5. The Rolling Pin shows Damage 16 and Reach 5.5. So the knife hits harder by 2 and the pin reaches farther by 1.0. It is a trade rather than a clear winner, which is why both sit in the lower tiers of our [best weapon list](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/best-weapon). The head-to-head is on the [kitchen knife vs rolling pin page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/kitchen-knife-vs-rolling-pin).

### Where you find them

The Rolling Pin was one of the first things on the shelves in a 13 Tips run. The Kitchen Knife turned up around Day 4 to 5 in two runs, and the prompt reads MELEE IMPACT when you swing it. Neither has a fixed spawn that we could confirm, so treat the days as examples.

### Against the Baseball Bat

The bat shows Damage 26 and Reach 8.0. Against the knife it is ahead by 8 damage and 3.5 reach, which the [bat vs knife page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/baseball-bat-vs-kitchen-knife) spells out. Against the pin it is ahead on both numbers too. So the stopgap advice is simple: carry one of the cheap ones until you find the bat, a hammer or a frying pan.

### The hotbar is the real limit

You carry four items plus a backpack slot, and those four are shared with water containers and tools. A weapon is the backup plan, not the plan. The game's pitch is staying quiet, and the survival book says noise travels. So the weapon you pick should be the one you are willing to swap out without regret. The [weapons page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/weapons) lists every card.

### Tools that double as weapons

Two objectives name a hammer, the Day 12 east fire door and the Stair B barrier, and the Hammer shows Damage 23 and Reach 5.0. If you find one, it is both a tool and a better weapon than either cheap option. The Adjustable Wrench shows Damage 16 and is needed for the roof hatch and the floor 27 tank, so you may carry it anyway.

### What we do not know

The cards do not show swing speed, durability or noise, and the game does not explain the unit of Reach. So this is a ranking of printed numbers. If a video shows a quiet weapon, we will say so. Until then, brace the door first, which the [Day 3 notes](https://www.tumblr.com/robloxgameguidesnow/833033588821934080) explain, and start from the [30 Floors Up guide](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/guide) if you are new.
