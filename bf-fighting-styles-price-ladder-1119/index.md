# Blox Fruits Fighting Styles: The Price Ladder From Dark Step to Godhuman

Fighting styles are the part of Blox Fruits that people buy once and keep. They are also priced on a clear ladder, which makes them easy to rank. There are 10 of them on sale right now, and the [Blox Fruits fighting styles price list](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/fighting-styles) shows every one in order.

## The ladder, top to bottom

- Godhuman: $5,000,000 Beli
- Sanguine Art: $5,000,000
- Dragon Talon: $3,000,000
- Electric Claw: $3,000,000
- Superhuman: $3,000,000
- Death Step: $2,500,000
- Sharkman Karate: $2,500,000
- Water Kung Fu: $750,000
- Electric: $500,000
- Dark Step: $150,000

Notice that the ten styles come in pairs and triples with the same price: two at $5M, three at $3M, two at $2.5M. That is why a trade between them is usually a straight swap on paper.

## Godhuman by the numbers

The [Godhuman value page](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/values/godhuman) says it ranks #5 of the 74 priced items, at $5,000,000 Beli, and that it was added in update 17.3. The page also lists a few fair trades worked out from the shop price. For example, 1 Godhuman is about 2 Death Step ($5M vs $5M) or 2 Gravity Fruit, and each of those counts as Fair because the two sides are 0.0% apart. It is not a promise that another player will accept it, only a check that the numbers line up.

## Where styles sit on the tier list

On the [Blox Fruits tier list](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/tier-list), tiers are set by fixed Beli cut-offs. Godhuman and Sanguine Art land in tier A, which runs from $3.2M to $8M. Dragon Talon, Electric Claw, Superhuman, Death Step and Sharkman Karate are tier B ($1M to $3.2M). Water Kung Fu and Electric are tier C, and Dark Step at $150,000 is also tier C because tier C starts at $100,000.

## Reading the list the smart way

1. A style that costs the same as a fruit is not necessarily worth the same to a buyer. Styles are a permanent upgrade to your moves, and fruits can be swapped any time.
2. If you want to compare styles to fruits, put both in the same list. The full [Blox Fruits value list](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/values) mixes them and lets you sort by value.
3. Always check the sync date. The site says values were synced on November 15, 2026, and every item page shows the same date.

## A note on what is not here

There is no Robux price shown for the fighting styles because the wiki does not list one. The site leaves that blank rather than guess. That is also true for other items without a listed price: they are marked as having no value yet.
