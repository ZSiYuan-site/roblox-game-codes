# SF BIKE codes, checked Nov 19 2026

SF BIKE keeps its codes in the Roblox description and pays cash rather than cosmetics, so the list is short and the values are easy to compare. The full list with sources is on the [SF BIKE codes page](https://newgamescout.com/sf-bike/codes).

1MILLIONVISITS is the largest payout at 8,500 Cash. The other two are 7,500 each, so redeeming all three is worth 23,500 Cash, which is enough to change what you can buy on day one.

## The spelling trap

NEWREALEASE, the launch code, has two Es. It is no longer in the description, so it sits in the may-have-expired group. Typing NEWRELEASE with one E always fails, which is why so many players think the code list is broken.

## How to redeem

1. Open SF BIKE on Roblox.
2. Click Settings in the button grid on the right.
3. Click Codes.
4. Paste the code into ENTER YOUR CODE and click Redeem.

Codes are case sensitive and the box does not trim a pasted trailing space. Copy rather than type.

## Where the cash goes

Bikes. The [Dragster update](https://newgamescout.com/sf-bike/new-bikes) added new bikes, and some of them are sold as passes instead of for cash, with prices on the [game passes page](https://newgamescout.com/sf-bike/game-passes). The free motorcycle is a group join reward, not a code, so do that separately.

The codes page is re-checked every day and shows how many are working, may have expired, and expired, so you can see the state without opening the game.
