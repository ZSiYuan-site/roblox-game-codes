# A Field Guide to Pips Answers

People looking up pips answers today and NYT Pips puzzle help usually want the same thing: a page that gives the answer without making them dig. A working checklist, organised by the question you are actually trying to answer rather than by the site's own menu. The pages below are all on Pips Answers and were picked because each one answers a different part of that question. None of them require an account, and none of them bury the useful part below a wall of text. If you only read one section, read the second one - that is the page most visitors are actually looking for. The rest are there for when the simple answer is not enough and you want to check the detail yourself.

## A good starting point

The [Pips answer archive](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/archive/) page is the place to start when the question is concrete: it puts the https: archive material on one screen instead of splitting it across a long list of posts. What makes it useful is the format. Every day has its own answer page, so a puzzle from last week does not get overwritten by today’s board. It is the sort of page you can scan in under a minute, then go back to when you need the exact figure or wording rather than a rough memory of it. A word of caution: the archive is only useful if the date is right; it is easy to open the wrong day when you are skimming. Treat the page as a working reference and re-open it when you need it, rather than trusting a note you made last week. That habit matters more here than it does on a static reference site. If this is the first page you have opened on Pips Answers, follow one of the links at the bottom before you leave. The site is built out of lots of small pages like this one, and the useful ones are rarely the homepage.

## Before you go

These four pages are a starting map, not the whole site. Pips Answers keeps adding and refreshing material, so the page that was newest when this was written will not be the newest one for long. The reliable approach is to pick the page that matches your question, check the date or the update note on it, and only then use the answer. Bookmark the one you come back to most often and ignore the rest - most readers need two or three pages, not all of them. If a link looks out of date, the [Pips Answers homepage](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/) is the quickest way back into the current material.
