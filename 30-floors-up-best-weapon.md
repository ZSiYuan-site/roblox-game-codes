# 30 Floors Up best weapon: quick pick list

A short pick list for the best weapon in [roblox 30 floors up](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up), based on item cards we read in player videos on Nov 9, 2026. The detailed page is [best weapon in 30 floors up](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/best-weapon).

## The pick order

1. Baseball Bat - damage 26, reach 8.0. Highest on both.
1. Hammer - damage 23, reach 5.0. Also an objective tool.
1. Frying Pan - damage 22, reach 6.0. One more reach than the Hammer.
1. Kitchen Knife - damage 18, reach 4.5.
1. Rolling Pin - damage 16, reach 5.5.
1. Adjustable Wrench - damage 16, reach not read.

## How to use the list in a run

Pick up anything with a Weapon tag, check the card, and keep the highest damage. Do not drop the Hammer, though: the Day 12 east fire door and the Stair B barrier both ask for one. The wrench is the same story, because it is needed to open the machine room hatch on the way to the roof and the north tank's riser valve in the office.

## Where the bat was seen

The Baseball Bat was held on Day 8 in one run and from Day 2 in a long run where it stayed in the hotbar until Day 38. The game pass descriptions also call a baseball bat the free starting weapon, but we have only seen it picked up in apartments, not handed out at the start.

## What is missing

The item card shows damage and reach only. Swing speed, durability and noise are unknown. Noise especially matters in a game about staying quiet, so a slow loud weapon may be worse than its numbers.

## A note on noise

Several objectives ask you to draw infected away before doing something loud, such as cutting a chain. That suggests the choice between a long-reach bat and a short knife may depend on the job as much as the numbers. We will test this as soon as a video makes the effect visible.
