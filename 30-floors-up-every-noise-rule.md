# Staying quiet in 30 Floors Up: every noise rule the game actually states

The pitch for [30 Floors Up Roblox](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up) is to stay quiet as zombies fill the hallways, and the game repeats it in dialogue, in the survival book and in its objectives. Instead of generic stealth advice, here are the noise rules the game states on screen, in the order you meet them.

### Night 0: Daniel's advice

When Daniel is let in on the first night, his advice is Keep the noise down and they stay put. That is the first rule and the simplest. It comes after the objectives that teach you to lock the entrance and check the peephole with R rather than opening the door to every knock.

### The survival book

Press B for the red book. Its Survival Rules include listen before opening a door and noise travels. There is also a line saying rules you learn the hard way will be written there, which suggests the book fills as you play. We have only seen the first two pages.

### Day 3: the corridor

The objective is Something is gathering in the corridor, then Brace the entrance. Drag the couch to the marked position and add more furniture. A chair can jam the handle. After the attack the game says furniture braces hold far better than a lock alone, so a barricade is quieter than a fight.

### Radios are noise

Mara's radio objective says to switch it on at low volume shortly before 19:00 and close the doors, because loud radios draw them in. The Day 12 and Day 14 objectives turn that around: a loud radio can lure zombies away from a corridor or a door. The Custom Music Radio pass is explicit that everyone nearby hears your music and so do the infected. See [Mara's radio and the 19:00 broadcast](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/neighbors).

### Running and the hammer

Infinite Stamina's description says running still makes noise. The Day 12 objective says a hammer on the east fire door is loud, so clear the corridor first. And the floor 27 fire door chain in the Day 31 to 32 objective is loud too, so draw them off first. See the [Day 9 to 38 objectives](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/later-days).

### Weapons and noise

The weapon cards show Damage and Reach only. We do not know noise per weapon, so we cannot rank by quietness. That gap is noted on the [best weapon page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/best-weapon).

### The habit to build

Peephole before unlock, chain on, radio low, hammer only when the corridor is clear. It is the same routine the opening teaches in the [30 Floors Up guide](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/guide) and the [controls page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/controls).
