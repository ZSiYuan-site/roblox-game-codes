# 30 Floors Up Day 9 to Day 38: the objectives and the one deadline

After the first week, <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up">30 floors up roblox</a> stops teaching and starts asking for things. This timeline lists the objectives we saw from Day 9 to Day 38 in the longest recorded run, a 53 minute video, and points out the only hard deadline we found.
<h3>Days 9 to 14: quiet, then crowds</h3>
Day 9 is a quiet day: ration carefully and check the guide's Supplies page. Day 10 asks you to inspect supplies, keep defences up and rest. Day 11 brings smoke over the river, with fires across the district, so keep the balcony door shut when it is windy. Day 12 asks you to close the east fire door with a hammer, which is loud, so clear the corridor first or lure the zombies past it. Day 13 is repairs and water. Day 14 warns that they move in groups: brace the entrance with furniture and boards, or leave a radio loud outside to draw them off.
<h3>Days 20 to 28: going out</h3>
Day 20 sends you to floor 28 to rescue Arthur Hale; bring a hammer or screwdriver and a bandage. Day 28 is the roof: lift 2's landing door, the shaft ladder, the machine room hatch and a wrench. You can read the details on the <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/roof-and-escape">roof and escape page</a>.
<h3>Days 31 to 38: down</h3>
Days 31 to 32 are Down Stair A: get into the management office on floor 27, open the north tank's riser valve with a wrench and cut the chain on the fire doors below. Days 34 to 36 are about the people below, and Day 38 is June's kitchen on floor 19.
<h3>The one deadline</h3>
One objective says to fix the Stair B barrier below 19 before Day 40. It is the only time limit we found, so plan to carry a hammer.
<ul><li>Keep a hammer and a wrench in the hotbar from about Day 12.</li><li>Check the objectives list every morning with Tab.</li><li>Rest every night; fatigue climbs through the day.</li></ul>
<h3>What we cannot tell you</h3><p>The timeline stops at Day 38 because that is where the longest video stops. We have not seen Day 40 or later, so we do not know what the barrier objective leads to, whether the rescue ever arrives, or how the days after 38 are structured. Each objective above comes from one or two videos, so wording may differ in your run.</p>
