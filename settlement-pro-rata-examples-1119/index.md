# How Pro Rata Shrinks a Settlement Payment: Three Real Examples

The phrase "up to" on a settlement page does a lot of work. It tells you the ceiling, not the check you will get. The reason is something called pro rata, and once you see it in three real settlements it is easy to read.

## What pro rata means

When more people claim than the fund can cover, every payment is cut by the same percentage. The [settlement payout timeline guide](https://settlements.resuhive.com/guides/settlement-payout-timeline/) puts it this way: a settlement that says "up to $50" may end up paying $12. Nobody is singled out. The fund is divided and the share per person falls as the number of claims rises.

## Example 1: Venetian Las Vegas, an estimate per booking

The [Venetian Las Vegas data privacy settlement](https://settlements.resuhive.com/venetian-las-vegas-data-privacy-california/) lists a fund of $931,713.50 and describes a pro rata share estimated at approximately $71.39 per booking. The word "estimated" is the important one: it is a guess made before the claims are counted. The claim deadline on the page is Nov 24, 2026, the eligible period runs Sep 18, 2024 to Mar 16, 2026, and the final approval hearing is Dec 3, 2026.

## Example 2: SeeEyeWear, a per-text ceiling

The [SeeEyeWear unwanted texts settlement](https://settlements.resuhive.com/seeeyewear-unwanted-texts/) offers up to $290 per text message, with a fund of $275,210. Those two numbers are worth reading together. A per-text figure sounds large, but a small fund and a large number of valid claims would push the payment down. The page also notes that the claim deadline is Nov 27, 2026 while the final approval hearing date listed is Nov 12, 2026, which means a hearing can come before a claim deadline.

## Example 3: Dr. Squatch, cents per product

The [Dr. Squatch all natural claims settlement](https://settlements.resuhive.com/dr-squatch-all-natural-claims/) pays $0.50 per product from a $9,000,000 fund. The official wording quoted on the page says the benefit is adjusted on a pro rata basis if valid claims add up to more than, or less than, the settlement amount. With proof of purchase you can claim up to 20 units; without it, up to 5. The claim deadline is Nov 27, 2026 and the final hearing is Mar 2, 2027.

## How to read an amount on any settlement page

1. Find the fund size. If the official site does not publish one, the page says so and does not guess.
2. Find the unit: per person, per text, per booking or per product.
3. Look for the words pro rata. If they are there, treat the number as a ceiling.
4. Look for tiers. The top number is often only for people with documented losses.
