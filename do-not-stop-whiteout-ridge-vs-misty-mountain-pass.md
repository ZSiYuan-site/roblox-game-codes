# Do Not Stop snow and mountain: Whiteout Ridge and Misty Mountain Pass compared

Two routes in Do Not Stop are not labelled with a difficulty on the wiki, because the label was cut off on screen: Misty Mountain Pass and Whiteout Ridge. They are also the two longest routes, so they are worth a short comparison. Everything below is from the wiki's notes.

Misty Mountain Pass is 13 miles. The card names The Shelf, the Old Baldy lifts and Devil's Elbow. It is map card 06. It was also the subject of the developer's Update 1 event, which ran from Nov 5 to 7 and made the pass harder with Steeple chasing you and new mysterious entities. The [Misty Mountain Pass page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps/misty-mountain-pass) covers it.

Whiteout Ridge is 16 miles, the longest listed. The card says: across the frozen lake, past the Outpost and up the switchbacks through a blizzard. It is card 07, and the Roblox badge is called Survived Whiteout Ridge. A frame from a player video on the home page shows it at mile 16 of 16, the last stretch before the shelter. The [Whiteout Ridge page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps/whiteout-ridge) has the details.

Put side by side: 13 miles against 16, a mountain road against a frozen lake and a blizzard, and an event that made one harder against a route that has only its card text. Neither has a difficulty label seen. The wiki lists them as Difficulty not seen rather than guess.

What does that mean for planning? The honest answer is that these are the two you should treat as unknown. Anything that tells you one is easier is not from this site. What the site can say is that they are the longest, which means more chances for the thing behind you to catch up and more mile signs to use as checkpoints.

Those mile signs matter more on a long route. The [Signal Lost page](https://donotstop.wiki/caught-and-retries) explains that caught after a mile sign means Retry from Mile takes you back to the last sign you passed. On a 16-mile route that is a big deal, since otherwise you rewind to the start.

For the snow, handling is the thing I would think about. N switches between Arcade and Sim, and the [controls page](https://donotstop.wiki/controls) lists it. The wiki does not claim that either is better on ice, and it has no data on grip on this map, so I won't either.

If you want to try them, work up from the Easy route. The [full maps list](https://donotstop.wiki/maps) shows the order and the [beginner guide](https://donotstop.wiki/beginner-guide) shows how to get through the first run. For a car to take, the [best cars page](https://donotstop.wiki/best-cars) has the ladder from D to S.

The tournament in Update 2 is said to use new terrain, according to the developer's event text, which is covered on the [Update 2 page](https://donotstop.wiki/update-2-tournament). It is not stated whether it uses these routes.

In short: Misty Mountain Pass, 13 miles, mountain road, Update 1 route. Whiteout Ridge, 16 miles, snow and blizzard. Both unlabelled for difficulty as of Nov 10, 2026.
