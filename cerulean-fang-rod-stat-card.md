# Cerulean Fang Rod: A Stat Card for Fisch Players

The Cerulean Fang Rod is a mid-to-late game rod in Fisch. Here is its stat card in one place.

## Cost and source

It costs 800,000 C$ and comes from the Luminescent Cavern. You need to reach the cavern and have the cash saved before you can buy it.

## Stats

- Luck: 190%
- Lure speed: 75%
- Control: 0.25
- Max weight: no limit

The lack of a weight limit is the standout stat: heavier fish can be landed without hitting a cap. The full [Cerulean Fang Rod](https://newgamescout.com/fisch/rods/cerulean-fang-rod) page compares it to nearby rods and explains where the cavern is.

## Who it is for

Players who have outgrown early rods and want consistent heavy-fish catches. Fisch data changes after updates, so the linked page is revised when the game changes.
