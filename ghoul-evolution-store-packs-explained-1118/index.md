# +1 Ghoul Evolution Store: what the Starter Pack and Pro Pack list

Clicker games like +1 Ghoul Evolution lean on a Store with time-limited boosts, and it is worth knowing what is listed before you decide to buy anything. This note reads the Store screen from a player video and points to the guide with the labelled frames. The free route, the codes, is on the [ghoul evolution codes](https://newgamescout.com/1-ghoul-evolution) page.

## The Starter Pack

In the FrostbiteGaming footage the Starter Pack lists +1 Blood Egg, +1K Wins and x2 Power for 15 minutes, with a price of 24. A Blood Egg is a pet egg, Wins unlock Kagunes, and the x2 Power timer lasts only a quarter of an hour. That timer is the part people waste. Start it right before a stage fight or a long punching-bag session.

## The Pro Pack

The Pro Pack lists +1 Celestial Egg, +30K Wins, x2 Power for 1 hour and +5% Power forever, with a price of 160. The permanent +5% is the one line in either pack that never expires, which makes it the more interesting purchase if you plan to stay. Whether it is worth the price is for you to judge; we are only reporting the listing.

## The small boosts

Above the boosts there is a pack icon, and other boosts sell for a small price: x2 Power and x2 Wins appear in the footage. The top of the screen also has a Rewards button and an Invite a Friend button, which are free routes to extra value.

## Free alternatives

Five codes are printed in the game description: 2MVISITS, GHOUL, WORLD3, THANKYOU and 2KCCU. The developer does not state what they give. Redeem them first, look at what changed, and only then decide whether a pack adds anything.

## How the progress curve affects value

In one clip a player is Level 6 with 450 Power; fifteen minutes later the account is Level 100 with 10.5M Power and a x45 multiplier. Because growth is so steep early on, a short x2 timer is worth far more when you are in the middle of a push than when you are idle. Stage 8 recommends 1.5M Power, so a timer spent just before it does real work.

## Reading the listing critically

Prices are shown in the footage in Robux and can change. We do not recommend spending; we only report. The [+1 Ghoul Evolution guide](https://newgamescout.com/1-ghoul-evolution) credits each frame to the video and the minute, so you can check what we say against the original.

Last point: Auto Rebirth is a toggle near the egg prompt. Learn what a rebirth resets before leaving it on, since packs and timers can be wasted on the wrong side of a reset.
