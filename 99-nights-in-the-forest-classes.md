# 99 Nights in the Forest classes: costs and starting items

Classes in 99 Nights in the Forest are bought with Diamonds, called Gems in some guides, from the Daily Class Shop in the lobby. The shop shows a random selection that changes every 24 hours, so the class you want may not be in stock today. Beebom says you get one free reroll the first time you play, and after that each reroll costs 99 Robux.

The cheapest class is Camper at 10 Diamonds, with a Flashlight. Scavenger costs 25 and starts with nothing. At 40 you can pick Base Defender (2 Defense Blueprints), Cook (Seasoning), Medic (2 Bandages), Hunter (2 Bear Traps) or Decorator (Hammer). Support is 45, Fisherman 50 with a Fishing Rod, and Gambler 55 with one random starter item.

In the middle range, Ranger at 70 starts with a Flashlight, a Revolver and 18 Revolver Ammo, and Lumberjack at 70 starts with a Good Axe and is rated highly by Robloxden. Farmer is 80 with a Watering Can. At 100 there are Alien (Ray Gun), Berserker (Medkit), Brawler (Leather Body) and Undead (Bandage). Chef costs 150, Blacksmith and Poison Master 200, and Fire Bandit 200 with an Infernal Crossbow.

The expensive end is Beastmaster at 400 with a Good Taming Flute, Assassin at 500 with a Katana and 120 Throwing Knives, and five classes at 600 each: Cyborg, Pyromaniac, Big Game Hunter, Necromancer and Vampire. Event classes such as Witch (900 Candies, Halloween) and Trick or Treater (30 Candies) are separate.

Once you own a class you keep it, and the perks come from levelling it up while you play, so a cheap class is not just a worse one. The two guides we cross-checked disagree on a few details, for example Zookeeper's starting item, so we left single-source classes out of the table.
