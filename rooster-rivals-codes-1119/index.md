# Rooster Rivals codes and the breeding loop they fund

Rooster Rivals is a Thai rooster sim: hatch chicks, feed the flock, breed for a Champion Bloodline and fight. Codes pay coins, titles and growth boosts, which map directly onto that loop. The full list is on the [Rooster Rivals code list](https://newgamescout.com/rooster-rivals).

## Six working codes

- TRADINGCHANGE: 10,000 coins
- 3MILLIONVISITS: 5,000 coins, a trophy, 2x XP and a title
- BLUEFACE: 1,500 coins, a Blueface egg, 2x Growth and a title
- 1MILLIONVISITS: 2,500 coins, a trophy and a title
- 4KVISITS: 1,500 coins, 2x Growth, Prime feed and a title
- 250ACTIVEUSERS: 2,500 coins, medicine and a title

Two more are listed as expired on the page. Four of the six hand out a title, which is cosmetic; the coins, trophy, XP, Growth and Prime feed are what change a farm.

## Why the Blueface egg matters

Blueface is the POWER breed added in Update 2.5, and the code is the only free way to get one. Growth boosts shorten the wait between hatches, so BLUEFACE and 4KVISITS are the two codes to redeem first if you are already breeding.

## The breeding loop

Pick a starter rooster from Bolt, Somtam and Tiger, then a hen. Eggs go into the Egg Box and only the ones you choose hatch. The Breeding Box takes one egg at a time. The [Rooster Rivals guide](https://newgamescout.com/rooster-rivals/guide) walks through the first hour, and it notes that the Breeding Preview shows ranges and chances, never an exact result.

## Update 2.5

The update adds Blueface on Spain, weekly clan versus clan Clan Derby fights, and player-hosted matches and cups at the Sparring Grounds. Ranked Arena, hen seasons, tournaments, the Gym Leader, a Trading Hub and visiting other farms are all named in the description. The [update log](https://newgamescout.com/rooster-rivals/update-log) dates each change.

Servers hold six players, so clans and the Derby are small-group work. Codes are re-checked daily.
