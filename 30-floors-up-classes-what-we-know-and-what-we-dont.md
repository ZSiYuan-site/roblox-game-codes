# 30 Floors Up classes: twelve names, three abilities we have actually read

Search for 30 Floors Up classes and most results give you a confident list of twelve abilities. We have only read three of them. This note separates what the shop shows from what we have not seen, so you do not buy or plan around a class that does not do what a copied list says. The overview of everything is on the [30 floors up roblox wiki](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up), and the [30 floors up classes page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/classes) holds the table.

## The count

The shop's Classes tab shows eleven classes, and the twelfth is Locksmith, which is a game pass at 1,299 Robux. So twelve names in total.

## The three we have read

- **Survivor.** The default class, marked EQUIPPED on its card. No bonuses, and nothing essential needs another class.
- **Plumber.** Collects water better: it fixes leaks and stuck valves faster and starts with a compact wrench. See the [Plumber page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/classes/plumber).
- **Locksmith.** From the Roblox pass description: you start each match with a screwdriver and nine bobby pins, and lockpicking is more forgiving. We have not seen a lockpicking scene yet. See the [Locksmith page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/classes/locksmith).

## The nine we have only seen as names

Scavenger, Trapper, Courier, Scout, Porter, Cook, Electrician, Caretaker and Medic. The cards were not open in the footage we checked, so we do not describe what they do. One third-party guide says a Caretaker's repaired door takes 15 percent less damage, but that comes from a guide, not from a card we have read.

## Does the class matter?

The game's tutorial runs on the starting items and the apartment. Nothing in Night 0 to Day 8 requires a class. Water, a bucket, a couch and a can opener do the work. The only reason to think about a class early is the Plumber, because the water chain on Days 2 to 6 involves a wrench and a seized valve; the [riser valve page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/riser-valve) shows what that looks like.

## The pass

Locksmith is one of ten passes. The cheapest is Shove at 99 Robux. Our [30 floors up game passes](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/game-passes) page lists all of them and what each one says it does.

## What would change this note

If you have opened a class card in the shop, tell us the name and the ability text. We will add it with a source. Until then, the safe assumption is that Survivor is fine.
