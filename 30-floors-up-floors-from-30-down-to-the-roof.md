# 30 Floors Up floors explained: what we know from floor 30 down to the roof

The 30 Floors Up title is literal. The Roblox description says you are stranded 30 floors above a zombie apocalypse, and your apartment is at the top. This note lists the floors that have appeared in objectives and player videos, and nothing else. We have not seen floors 1 to 10, 12 to 18 or 20 to 25, or the ground floor, and we will not invent them.

## Floor 30: home

Your flat and the hallway outside. Doors are numbered 30xx, and 3002 belongs to a neighbor. The hallway has a cork board, a barred gate and the landing door of lift 2. A caption near the lift reads slow pounding behind the 29 landing door. The full list is on the [30 Floors Up floors](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/floors) page.

## Floor 28: Arthur Hale

The Day 20 objective sends you down lift 2's shaft to the 28 landing door, then the fire doors, because Stair A is packed. You need to get Arthur Hale out of the maintenance room and bring a hammer or a screwdriver and a bandage. He has no water left after Day 21.

## Floor 27: the management office

Days 31 to 32 are called Down Stair A. Get into the office with Arthur's key or by forcing the door, open the north tank's riser valve with a wrench, and cut the chain on the fire doors below 27. That is loud, so draw the zombies off first. A service door in this office leads to the shaft and Stair B.

## Floor 26: a landing

Arthur left something here on Day 32, and a family note signed the Haddads, 2604 is pinned in a crate. Stair A is burnt below this landing.

## Floor 19: June's kitchen

The Day 38 objective names the route: the service door in the 27 office, the shaft ladder and Stair B. Bring June two empty water containers, and fix the Stair B barrier below 19 before Day 40 with a hammer and a board. Everything from Day 9 on is laid out on the [Day 9 to 38 objectives](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/later-days) page.

## Floor 11: the clinic

Named in the Day 34 to 36 objective The people below. It has not been shown on screen in the footage we checked.

## The roof

The Day 28 objective is the roof: open lift 2's landing door on 30, climb the shaft ladder, unclip the gate and shove the loose panel aside, undo the machine room hatch with a wrench, then go out through the machine room door. The Update 2.0 description adds rooftop shelters. In the footage you carry boards up, set them on the glowing outlines and nail each piece; the game says The rooftop anchor stands, and a Cold meter fills while you are up there. The [30 Floors Up rooftop shelter](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/rooftop-shelter) page has the materials, and the [rooftop garden](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/rooftop-garden) page covers the planters: seeds take six days and each bed needs 1 L of water.

## Why the order matters

Each floor is a chain of small jobs rather than free roaming, and each objective names the tool you need: a key, a wrench, a hammer or a board. Carry the right tool before you take the lift. We do not know whether floors change between campaigns.
