# Raise The Biggest Family: The Free First Hour, Step by Step

The first hour of [Raise The Biggest Family](https://newgamescout.com/raise-the-biggest-family) is a tidy little loop: drive, interview, adopt, drive home, name the baby. This page follows that loop using the on-screen text recorded by New Game Scout from two player videos, and shows where Robux prompts appear so you can skip them.

## Before you start

The game is a roleplay life sim by Novkod and Level0Expert, created Oct 13, 2026 and last updated Oct 31, 2026. Servers hold up to 18 players. Per the game description your children keep aging while you are logged out, which is why the page tells you to come back daily.

## Step by step

- Walk out of your house to the car and drive to the adoption center. The drive has a countdown bar, for example 0:43 or 0:33 to the center.
- Do not press Skip Time. In one video it opened a Robux prompt for Skip to Adoption, so the plain drive is the free route.
- Join the queue marked by pink ropes. A fee board on the wall lists the cost of each baby: the first is free, then 100 coins, 300, 750, 1,500 and 3,000 for the sixth.
- Answer the three timed check-in questions. Each has a countdown and three numbered answers.
- Pass the adoption interview in the same format; the first question asks how you will look after your baby.
- Visit the nursery, buckle the baby into the car and drive home in the dark.
- Name the child. The family counter changes from 0/3 to 1/3.
- Place the baby mat in the nursery when prompted.

## The screen

The left side has Shop, Rewards, Tasks and Family buttons, with counters for family, gems and coins. One account showed 50 gems and 350 coins before the first adoption; a second started with about 10 coins. These are starting values for those runs, not guarantees. The right edge has Skip to Adopt and two Grow All buttons, marked +1 Hour and +5 Hours. The site only saw their names, so it does not say what they do.

## Care

A board titled Your Children Need You lists hungry, sleepy, dirty, bored, sick and homework. The growth chart read from the wall says a toddler arrives after one hour, age 3 after four hours, age 5 after twelve, a teen on day 2, an adult on day 7 and age 20 on day 11. Hold a baby and you get Dress and Put Down; the Formula Station has a Grab Bottle prompt on E.
