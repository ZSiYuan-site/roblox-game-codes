# Do Not Stop: East Hollow Way and All Souls Road, the two Hard routes with names

Of the seven routes in Do Not Stop, three are marked Hard on the maps screen. Two of them, East Hollow Way and All Souls Road, have named places on their cards, which makes them easy to describe. Here is what the wiki records for each.

East Hollow Way is 11 miles. The map card runs from Harrow's Patch through the Maze and the Harvest Fair, under a red sky. The developer's Halloween event names it, with text saying Steeple has followed you after you escaped from East Hollow Way. It is the map card number 04, and there is a Roblox badge for it. The [East Hollow Way page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps/east-hollow-way) has the details.

One loading tip on this map is the best explanation of the passenger role in the game. It says: Passenger? Look down at the scanner. It shows the road ahead, and the Runner in red when it's near. So if you are driving with a friend, this is the map where the radar job is spelled out.

All Souls Road is also marked Hard. The card goes from the Bone Orchard past Widow's Mere and St Vigil's. It is card 05 on the maps screen and has its own Roblox badge. The wiki says it has not read the mile total. The [All Souls Road page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps/all-souls-road) says the same thing and notes that there is no screenshot yet.

There is a gap here that I like seeing written down. For All Souls Road, the miles are listed as not shown in game. For Highway Zero, the third Hard route, the miles are not shown either and the wiki says it was missing from the six-map walkthrough video, so it hasn't been seen driven. See the [Highway Zero page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps/highway-zero) for what is known.

So how do you decide between them? On paper, East Hollow Way is the one with more information: 11 miles, three named stops and a developer event. All Souls Road is a mystery on distance. If you are using the numbers to plan, remember that the [maps table](https://donotstop.wiki/maps) lets you sort by miles and filter by difficulty, and that the missing values are marked rather than guessed.

If you are not ready for Hard, the usual route is up from the start. Route 1610 is the Easy one at 7 miles, and County Road 13 is Medium at 9 miles. See the [medals page](https://donotstop.wiki/medals-and-records) for the only medal times recorded so far.

Before a Hard route, I would check the keys. Space for the handbrake and drift is the main one, and the [controls page](https://donotstop.wiki/controls) explains that and the Arcade or Sim switch. A caught run sends you to the Signal Lost screen, which the [retries page](https://donotstop.wiki/caught-and-retries) covers, including how mile signs act as checkpoints.

That is the summary: two Hard routes with great names, one with 11 miles and one with no mileage on record yet. Both are on the wiki and both are marked as checked on Nov 10, 2026.
