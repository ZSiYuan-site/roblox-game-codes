# Do Not Stop Codes, Maps and Cars: A Starter Overview

Do Not Stop is a Roblox driving and obstacle game. Six codes are working as of November 2026. This page gives the starter context around them.

## The codes

Six codes are currently active. The [Do Not Stop codes](https://donotstop.wiki/) wiki lists them with the last-checked date and a redemption walkthrough.

## The seven maps

The game has seven maps. Each one changes the obstacles and the route, so learning the layouts matters as much as having codes. The wiki lists all seven.

## The five cars

Cars are grouped into five grades. Higher grades handle obstacles better, so progression is tied to unlocking better vehicles. The wiki shows the grades and how they compare.

## Controls and credits

The wiki also covers the controls and credits the game shows, so you know what each input does before a run. Codes, maps, cars and controls are all in one place there.
