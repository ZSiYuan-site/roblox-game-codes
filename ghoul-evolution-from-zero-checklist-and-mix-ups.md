# +1 Ghoul Evolution: A First-Session Checklist and the Name Mix-Up to Avoid

Searching for a ghoul game on Roblox is confusing because several titles share the theme. This checklist is for people who landed on [+1 Ghoul Evolution](https://newgamescout.com/1-ghoul-evolution) and want to know what it is, what to do first, and what not to confuse it with. All facts come from the game's page on New Game Scout.

## What the game is

+1 Ghoul Evolution is a clicker-style power game by smile for ccu, released on Roblox on Oct 26, 2026 and last updated Nov 11, 2026. You click to gain +1 Power, train on punching bags for bigger boosts, fight enemies, clear stages and collect Wins to unlock new Kagunes. You can also hatch pets, collect auras and rebirth to get stronger. Power keeps accumulating while you are offline. Servers hold up to 12 players. The genre label is Simulation, incremental simulator.

## The mix-up

The site's "Not to be confused with" line names Ro-Ghoul and Ghoul://RE. Those are different Roblox games. Codes for them will not work here, and guides for them will not describe this game's Kagunes, pets or Wins. If a video thumbnail says Ro-Ghoul, it is probably not about this game.

## First-session checklist

- Redeem the four listed codes: GHOUL, WORLD3, THANKYOU and 2KCCU. Rewards are not stated anywhere reliable, so expect to find out in game. The steps are on the [codes page](https://newgamescout.com/1-ghoul-evolution/codes).
- Click for Power and train on punching bags for bigger boosts.
- Clear early stages and collect Wins; Wins unlock Kagunes.
- Hatch pets and equip auras when you reach them.
- Consider rebirth once progress slows, since it resets you to become stronger.
- Log out with confidence: you keep gaining Power while away.
