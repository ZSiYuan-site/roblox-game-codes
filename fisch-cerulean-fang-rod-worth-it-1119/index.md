# Is the Cerulean Fang Rod Worth 800,000 C$ in Fisch?

The Cerulean Fang Rod in Fisch costs 800,000 C$, has no weight limit, and sits in the middle of the luck table. Whether it is worth buying depends on which rods you are comparing it with. This note uses only the figures on the rod page.

## The stats

The [Cerulean Fang Rod](https://newgamescout.com/fisch/rods/cerulean-fang-rod) page lists: Luck 190%, Lure speed 75%, Control 0.25, Resilience 35% and Max weight: No limit. It is a permanent rod that comes from Luminescent Cavern. Ranked against the rest of the wiki data, luck is #42 of 186 rods, lure speed is #80 of 184, control is #15 of 186, and max weight is #1 of 186 because there is no limit at all.

## What it is competing with

The page builds a comparison from rods in the same area and the nearest permanent rods by luck:

| Rod | Price (C$) | Luck % | Lure % | Control | Max kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fortune Rod | 11,000 | 200 | 30 | 0.05 | 3,000 |
| Ethereal Prism Rod | 3,500,000 | 195 | 95 | 0.25 | 250,000 |
| Cerulean Fang Rod | 800,000 | 190 | 75 | 0.25 | no limit |
| Kraken Rod | 950,000 | 185 | 60 | 0.2 | 115,000 |
| Rainbow Cluster Rod | 250,000 | 180 | 65 | 0 | 50,000 |
| Lucky Rod | 4,500 | 177 | 20 | 0.07 | 175 |

Two things jump out. The Fortune Rod has more luck (200 vs 190) and costs 11,000 C$, but it caps at 3,000 kg. And the Ethereal Prism Rod beats the Cerulean Fang on every number except price, which is 3.5 million.

## So when does it make sense

The honest answer from the table: the Cerulean Fang is a weight-limit rod. Its edge over Kraken Rod is 5 more luck, 15 more lure speed, 0.05 more control and no limit, for 150,000 C$ less. If you have been losing big fish because the line snaps at the weight cap, that is the reason to buy it. If you are only chasing luck, the cheaper rods get you nearly the same percentage.

## Check the fish before you pay

The rod page puts it plainly: it pays for itself only if it lets you land fish you could not before. Look at the heaviest fish you want in the [Fisch fish list](https://newgamescout.com/fisch/fish) and compare its weight to the rod's cap. For the cost side, price a typical catch in the [Fisch sell price calculator](https://newgamescout.com/fisch/calculator) to see how many catches it takes to earn back 800,000 C$.

## Common mistakes

1. Buying for luck alone when a rod costing a fifth as much has nearly the same percentage.
2. Ignoring resilience. It is 35% here, against 40% on the Ethereal Prism.
3. Treating the rank as absolute. Rank counts only the rods that have that stat in the wiki data, and ties share a place.
