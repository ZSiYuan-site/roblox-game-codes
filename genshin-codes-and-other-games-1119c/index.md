## Why the check time matters

Codes are case-sensitive and most work once per account, and they expire without warning. Each page keeps an expired list so you can tell a bad code from a typo. The [all games index](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/games/) lists every title covered; the site also tracks Tacticus, Arknights Endfield, Wuthering Waves and others. Copy codes rather than typing them, and check the timestamp before trusting a list.
