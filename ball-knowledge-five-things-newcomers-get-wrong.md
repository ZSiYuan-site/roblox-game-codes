# Ball Knowledge: Five Things Newcomers Get Wrong About a Trivia Party Game

[Ball Knowledge](https://newgamescout.com/ball-knowledge) looks like a normal quiz, so people play it like one. That is the first mistake. Below are five misreadings, each checked against the game description summarised on New Game Scout.

## 1. Playing to be right instead of rare

Everyone at the table gets the same question and types one answer. Common answers score a little and rare answers score a lot. In the description's example, Dog is worth 10 points and Fennec Fox is worth 90, and Legendary is worth 100. A correct but obvious answer earns almost nothing.

## 2. Expecting instant results

Answers are revealed together, from Common up to Legendary, and the match runs for 7 rounds. The highest total after round 7 wins. There is no single big question; the lead is made in small differences across rounds.

## 3. Assuming there is only one mode

The game lists 1v1 duels, 3-player battles, 4-player tables and an 8-player main stage. The server cap is 30 players. The crowd you face changes your strategy: in a 1v1 duel there is only one other person who might have typed the same answer.

## 4. Hunting for codes

The description mentions no codes, and the site checked on Nov 13, 2026 and listed none. Coins come from matches and a free daily reward, and they buy chairs, thrones and answer effects. Spend time on rounds instead of searching.

## 5. Judging it by the like bar

The like rate is 66.5 percent (1,477 likes against 745 dislikes), lower than the 80 percent mark the site calls a sign players like a game. But 1.89 million visits and 3,464 players at the last pull show people are playing.

## Quick facts

Developer: Gray Games Official. Released Oct 14, 2026. Last updated Nov 9, 2026. Genre: party and trivia. Questions: 135 or more, spanning animals, food, countries, sports, science, music and video games. Favorites: 8,353. ## A tiny plan

- Pick a niche answer you are fairly sure about.
- Avoid the first thing everyone would say.
- Save coins for a chair or an answer effect you actually want.
