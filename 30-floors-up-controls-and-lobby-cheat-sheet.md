# 30 Floors Up controls and lobby cheat sheet: keys, bars and the Create a Group window

A cheat sheet for the two screens you meet first in 30 Floors Up: the lobby where you create a group, and the controls once you are inside the apartment. Facts are from the Roblox page and player videos, and anything not shown is marked as unknown. For the full explanation of each key, see the [30 Floors Up controls](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/controls) page.

## Keys

E picks up, opens or talks. F eats or drinks when held, and also unlocks the front door. The number keys 1 to 4 take an item from your four slots, Q puts the held item away and X drops it. R looks through the peephole and T hooks the door chain. Tab hides or shows the objectives list, and B opens the red survival book. H shoves, but only if you own the Shove pass.

## The five bars

Health, Hunger, Thirst, Fatigue and Stamina sit at the bottom left. Hunger and thirst drop over time, fatigue climbs through the day, and you sleep in the bed, couch or mattress you chose on Night 0. Stamina is used for running. In one run health read 67 and later 42, so it moves in the first day. Bandages turn up in the apartments.

## The front door routine

Facing the door gives three actions: unlock with F, peephole with R, chain with T. The Night 0 objective asks you to check the peephole before you answer, and it is a good habit for every knock. The survival book repeats the rule: listen before opening a door, because noise travels.

## Create a Group

In the lobby, step on an empty pad that reads Available, 1-4 players. The Create a Group window has four choices. Players is 1 to 4. Difficulty is Easy, Normal or Hard, and Normal is labelled the intended balance and recommended. Access is Public or Friends only, which means only the host's Roblox friends can join. Campaign is New saved game or Temporary, which plays without saving. We have not seen what Easy or Hard change. The [30 Floors Up difficulty and lobby](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/difficulty-and-lobbies) page keeps track of that.

## Save slots

A saved campaign is the choice for a long run, since objectives go past Day 38. Save Slot 2 and Save Slot 3 are 999 Robux game passes and add more campaign slots; the server itself holds up to 30 players.

## Water in one line

Fill the bucket in the bathtub, put it on the living-room water rack, and keep Treatment Tablets with it. The tablets were in the bathroom mirror cabinet on Night 0, tap water you store shows as clean, and the rainwater you collect on Day 6 is flagged untreated. Read the item panel before holding F. We have not seen the prompt for using the tablets, so we give no button for it. See [how to treat water in 30 Floors Up](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/treat-water).

## Rations in one line

A full ration is two portions and 2 L of water per person per day, according to the in-game guide, and the goal is 100 days. Tins count as two portions and need a can opener. If you want the arithmetic done for you, use the [30 Floors Up ration planner](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/ration-planner).
