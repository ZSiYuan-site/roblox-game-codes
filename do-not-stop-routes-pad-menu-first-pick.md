# Do Not Stop: seven routes, one pad menu and what to pick first

[Do Not Stop](https://donotstop.wiki/) is a Roblox driving game in which something chases you the whole way to a shelter at the end of the road. The lobby offers seven routes, and the first question new players ask is which one to start on. This note sticks to what the game and player videos show.

### The pad menu

You step on a pad in the lobby, pick a route and a passenger, and press Drive Alone or invite someone to read the radar. The [beginner guide](https://donotstop.wiki/beginner-guide) walks through that in order. The controls are W and S to drive and brake, A and D to steer, and Space to handbrake and drift. The loading screen says to hit Space to drift around corners, and the full key list is on the [controls page](https://donotstop.wiki/controls).

### Route 1610 first

Route 1610 is the one marked Easy on the maps screen. It begins with a gas station on the right, and in the [Route 1610 page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps/route-1610) you can see the layout. There is also a gold medal on this map, which the [medals and records page](https://donotstop.wiki/medals-and-records) discusses.

### The harder ones

Misty Mountain Pass is the route Update 1 made harder with Steeple chasing you, and Whiteout Ridge is the snowy one at the end of the list, with mile 16 of 16 shown as the last stretch before the shelter. The [maps page](https://donotstop.wiki/maps) lists all seven with their difficulty, and the [Steeple page](https://donotstop.wiki/steeple) covers what the game says about the thing behind you.

### Cars

The garage has five cars graded D to S. You start in the Veltrano 4WD Turbo, Grade D, with 4 out of 10 on Speed, Accel, Grip and Brakes. The Hartmann 220 Sport unlocks at level 4 and the Branca at level 20. We rank by the printed grade only; see [best cars](https://donotstop.wiki/best-cars).

### Codes

Two codes were checked on November 11: RELEASE for 250 credits and DONOTSTOP for 200. LASTSTOP and ROUTE1610 are in the game description too, but we have not seen them entered. Open the Garage, the Codes tab, type and press Redeem. The [codes page](https://donotstop.wiki/codes) has the current status. Last Stop is a different Roblox game, so its codes do not work here.

### What changes on November 14

The developer's events list shows Update 2 plus Tournament on November 14 at 17:00 UTC, with exclusive prizes. Endless Mode is listed as coming very soon, and we have not seen it in play. The [update 2 and tournament page](https://donotstop.wiki/update-2-tournament) keeps the countdown.

### Our suggestion

Start on Route 1610 with Arcade handling, learn to drift, then move up. The whole site is indexed at [donotstop.wiki](https://donotstop.wiki/).
