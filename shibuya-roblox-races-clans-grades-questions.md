# Shibuya Roblox questions answered: races, clans, grades and the first technique

Shibuya is a Roblox open-world fighter inspired by Jujutsu Kaisen, set 1,000 years after the original story. It is free to play, in Beta, and has hardcore elements: the stronger you get, the more a careless death can cost you. Up to 25 players share a server. Here are the questions newcomers ask most, answered from the Roblox description, the creation screen in player videos and two wiki sources. Nothing was tested by us in-game.

## What are the odds on the creation screen?

The screen lists them. Races: Death Painting is Mythic at 2 percent, Mutated Corpse is Epic at 4, Incarnated Sorcerer is Rare at 7, and Human and Cursed Spirit are Common at 87 together. Clans: Itadori, Okkotsu and Zenin are Mythic at 0.25 percent, Gojo, Hakari, Kamo, Kashimo and Tsukumo are Legendary at 1, and the Rare tier is 10. One wiki calls Mutated Corpse Legendary, so sources disagree. Pick a clan for its bonus, not its rarity. The full table is on the [Shibuya races and clans](https://newgamescout.com/shibuya/races-and-clans) page.

## How do I get more rerolls?

From codes and from Robux in the customization menu; shibuya.wiki found no reliable free farming. Race, Clan and Cursed Technique rerolls change those traits, while Face, Hair Color, Theme, Cursed Energy Color and Race Variant rerolls are cosmetic. Unique techniques cannot be rerolled. Codes are redeemed in the Redeem Code box at the top right of the customize screen. Two codes, PUBLICTESTING and RANKEDFIXES, are the ones at least two sources agree on, as of November 15. Check the [Shibuya codes](https://newgamescout.com/shibuya/codes) page for the dated list.

## How do I rank up?

Pass Grade Exams at the red phone booth. The panel shows your grade and the next exam, in Free For All or Gauntlet modes; press E to enter and C to close. One guide says to finish first, since second place fails. Grades 5 to 3 are mostly hunting and containing curses, and at Grade 2 you become eligible for a cursed technique. See [Shibuya sorcerer grades](https://newgamescout.com/shibuya/sorcerer-grades).

## How do I earn yen?

Mostly by containing curses. Beat a curse, hold E on the contain prompt until it says contained, and hand it in to Agent Hoshino at HQ. Containment Ofuda cost about 250 yen a sheet in one guide.

## How do I get a cursed technique?

Reach Grade 2 first. Buy the gas mask from Tomoe in the subway for 5,000 yen, pay Jira 10,000 yen at the desert gate, rent Akone's bike through the ravine, plant the relay and clear the inner trial. Yuki Sato then sells techniques: Slicing Exorcism 12,000, Piercing Blood 25,000 and Supernova 40,000 yen. The steps are on the [Shibuya cursed techniques](https://newgamescout.com/shibuya/cursed-techniques) page.

## How do I block and parry?

Hold F to block and tap F right before a hit to parry. There is no separate parry key and the timing window is not published. Other keys: M1 attack, Q dash, E infuse cursed energy, X uppercut, L equip weapon.

## Do codes from Project Mugetsu work?

No. Project Mugetsu is a different Roblox game with its own codes. If you want a ground-up first hour instead, the [Shibuya beginner guide](https://newgamescout.com/shibuya/beginner-guide) covers it.
