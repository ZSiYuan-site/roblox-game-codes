# Bible Trivia for Kids: Ten Stories and the Verses Behind Them

Kids' Bible trivia works best when every answer points back to a verse. The [Bible trivia for kids](https://trivia.resuhive.com/bible/kids/) set does that: ten questions, each with the book and chapter in the question and a short line of explanation. This is a walk through all ten.

## Genesis

1. Who built the ark to survive the flood (Genesis 6)? Noah. The explanation gives the size from Genesis 6:15: 300 cubits long, 50 wide and 30 high.
2. On which day of the creation week did God rest (Genesis 2)? The seventh day, from Genesis 2:2.
3. Who is the first man named in the Bible (Genesis 2)? Adam, who Genesis 2:7 says was formed from the dust of the ground.
4. What bird did Noah send out that came back with an olive leaf (Genesis 8)? A dove. Genesis 8:11 says it returned in the evening with a freshly plucked olive leaf, a sign the waters were receding.

## Stories from the prophets and the kings

5. Who spent three days and three nights inside a great fish (Jonah 1)? Jonah. The book says the Lord provided the great fish.
6. Which giant did young David defeat with a sling and a stone (1 Samuel 17)? Goliath. David picked up five smooth stones from a stream first.
7. Who was thrown into a den of lions but was not harmed (Daniel 6)? Daniel. In Daniel 6:22 he says God sent an angel to shut the lions' mouths.

## Exodus and the New Testament

8. Who led the Israelites out of Egypt (Exodus 12-14)? Moses. Exodus 14 describes the sea dividing so that they could cross on dry ground.
9. In which town was Jesus born, according to Luke 2? Bethlehem. Joseph and Mary travelled there for a census.
10. How many apostles did Jesus choose from among his disciples (Luke 6)? Twelve, from Luke 6:13.

## How to use it with children

- Ask the question without the verse reference first, then give the reference as a hint if they are stuck.
- After the answer, open the verse and read it aloud. The set cites the verse so you can do this.
- Do one question a night rather than all ten at once if the kids are young.
