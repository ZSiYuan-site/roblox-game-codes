# Pips October 13, 2025: The Day the Hard Board Was the Smallest

Most Pips days follow a pattern: Easy is the smallest, Hard the biggest. October 13, 2025 broke it. On the [Pips answer for October 13, 2025](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/answers/2025-10-13/) page, the three boards used 6, 6 and 7 dominoes. That is the smallest Hard board in the sample dates we checked, and it is worth a closer look.

## The three boards

- Easy: 6 dominoes, built by Rodolfo Kurchan. 1 sum, 1 not-equal, 2 equal and 1 greater-than regions.
- Medium: 6 dominoes, built by Heidi Erwin. 2 greater-than, 3 sum and 1 equal regions.
- Hard: 7 dominoes, built by Rodolfo Kurchan. 2 greater-than, 1 sum and 3 equal regions.

Easy, Medium and Hard are the labels, but by size alone you could not tell them apart. Easy and Medium use the same number of dominoes.

## Why Hard was still Hard

Hard had only one sum region and three equal regions. That sounds easy, but equal regions with many cells need doubles, and a set of 7 dominoes has few doubles to spare. Hint 2 on the Hard board said to start with the region at row 2, column 4, where all the cells match: it is a 1. Hint 3 placed the 5-6 domino at the 5 in row 2, column 2 and the 6 in row 1, column 2.

## The Easy board hint

Easy also began with an all-equal region. Hint 2 said the region at row 3, column 2 is a 0, and Hint 3 placed the 5-3 domino at the 5 in row 5, column 1 and the 3 in row 4, column 1. A not-equal region is the rare piece here: every half in it has to be different.

## Compare with a modern board

In November 2026, Hard boards use 16 dominoes, more than double that size. For comparison, the [Pips answer for November 16, 2026](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/answers/2026-11-16/) page shows 16 dominoes and 9 greater-than regions. The board for October 13, 2025 was a very different kind of puzzle.

## What to learn

1. Size is only part of difficulty. The region mix matters as much.
2. Start with the equal regions, as both hints did that day.
3. Use old boards for practice. The [archive](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/archive/) has all 458, and small ones are a gentle way to learn the symbols.
