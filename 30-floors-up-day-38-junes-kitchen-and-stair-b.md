# 30 Floors Up Day 38: June's kitchen, two water containers and the Stair B barrier

The deepest objective anyone has shown in [30 Floors Up Roblox](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up) so far is Day 38, in a 53 minute run. It takes you to floor 19, asks you to bring water to a stranger and sets a deadline two days later. This note lays that objective out in plain steps.

### Where June is

June runs a community kitchen on floor 19. By the Day 34 to 36 objectives, the plans and the family note point further down the building toward her kitchen and a clinic on floor 11. Stair A is blocked below 26, so you cannot just walk down. The [floors page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/floors) shows where each of these sits in the building.

### Three ways down

The Day 38 objective names the route: the service door in the floor 27 office, the shaft ladder, and Stair B. The office is the management office from the Day 31 to 32 objective, where you open the north tank's riser valve with a wrench. The service door in that office leads to the shaft and to Stair B.

### What June asks for

Bring June two empty water containers. That is the whole request, and it is a callback to the water routine from Night 0, where the first job was filling a bucket. If you kept the spare buckets, bottles and the 20 L Jerrycan from the early days, you will have them. The [items page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/items) lists the container sizes.

### The deadline

The same objective says to fix the Stair B barrier below 19 before Day 40, with a hammer and a board. So the hammer you carried for the Day 12 fire door comes back again. The Day 38 run ended around there, so we have not seen what happens at Day 40.

### What to carry

A hammer, a board, two empty water containers and enough food to last a couple of days. Your hotbar is four slots plus a backpack, so leave the heavy weapon choices for the [best weapon page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/best-weapon). A wrench is also handy for the floor 27 tank if you have not done it yet.

### Where this leaves the story

This is the going-down thread of the game, separate from the roof thread on Day 28. We have not seen either reach a rescue or Day 100. If you want the whole arc, the [Day 9 to 38 objectives](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/later-days) page has it in order, and the [roof and escape page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/roof-and-escape) covers the other way.
