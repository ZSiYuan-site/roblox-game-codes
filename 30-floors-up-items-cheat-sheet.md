# 30 Floors Up items cheat sheet

Every item in [30 Floors Up on Roblox](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/items) has a tag on its card: WATER, TOOL, WEAPON, COOKING, KEY or MATERIAL. Reading the tag first saves time, because it tells you whether the thing goes on the water rack, into your hotbar, or can safely be ignored. This sheet organises the items by what you do with them, using only things seen in player videos. The full table is on the [items page](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/items).

## Water items

The Cleaning Bucket holds 10 L, starts empty and is carried in both hands. The 20 L Jerrycan shows 0.0 / 20 L when empty, with the prompt Scoop water into the container in the bathtub. The 1 L Bottle starts empty; the Sealed 2 L Bottle shows 1.5 / 2 L and is marked clean and safe to drink. Hold F to drink, and one sip in the MBT video was 0.5 L. Tap water you store on Night 0 shows as clean, rainwater does not, so [how to treat water](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/treat-water) is the follow-up read.

## Tools

The Screwdriver is for screws: the prompt Undo the screw appears at the vent cover, followed by Kick the cover out (2/3). The Toolbox shows up in a tip from the 13 Tips video, bring it and open the vent door to reach a second room. Bolt Cutters were seen on Day 4 and Day 5 next to the objective to inspect supplies and keep defences up. The Battery Radio is a TOOL too: F switches it on or off, holding T fits fresh batteries, and it shows a battery percentage and the North Relay Band.

## Cooking, keys and materials

The Portable Gas Stove is tagged COOKING and needs both hands. Tinned food such as Tinned Tomatoes (2 portions) needs a can opener, or the Spoon game pass. The Small Brass Key is tagged KEY, but the only locked door seen near it was labelled as the Okafors' private rooms, nothing you need in there, so do not burn time on it. The Static Line (40 m) is a MATERIAL carried in both hands.

## Slots

You get four numbered slots (keys 1 to 4) plus a backpack slot. Q puts an item away and X drops it. Because both-hands items like the bucket and the stove take the whole hand, plan your trips: fill, carry, drop, then pick the next thing.

## A short list of habits

Keep Treatment Tablets with the rainwater bucket. Keep a screwdriver or the toolbox on you before opening vent covers. Switch the radio off between broadcasts. Check the tag before picking something up.
