# 30 Floors Up first night water plan: bucket, rack and tablets

The first real job in <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up">30 floors up roblox</a> is not fighting anything. It is carrying water. The Night 0 objectives and the first page of the survival book both say the same thing: store water before the taps stop. This note turns that into a short plan you can follow in about ten minutes, using only what shows up in player footage.
<h3>Step one: the bucket</h3>
The Cleaning Bucket is a blue 10 L container in the cabinet under the kitchen sink. The objectives read: pick up the bucket, place it inside the bathtub, then turn on the water and fill it. The bathtub gives separate prompts for the cold tap, the hot tap and the plug, and the item panel shows the water level. When it says Full, turn the tap off, pick the bucket up (it needs both hands) and carry it to the metal rack in the living room.
<h3>Step two: more containers</h3>
On Day 2 the objective becomes fill every container before the taps stop, and a counter shows stored clean water; one run read 141 L. That means one bucket is not enough. The 20 L Jerrycan shows 0.0 / 20 L when empty, and the 1 L and 2 L bottles are small but useful for carrying. See the <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/items">items page</a> for sizes and tags.
<h3>Step three: know which water is clean</h3>
Tap water you store on Night 0 shows as clean, a bucket read 10.0 L clean on Day 2. Rainwater is different: the Day 6 objective asks you to set an open bucket on a balcony bracket, and the game adds that rainwater is untreated. That is the job of Treatment Tablets, found in the bathroom mirror cabinet on Night 0 and again beside the water bottles on Day 6. We have not seen the exact prompt for using them, so the <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/treat-water">treat water page</a> says so plainly.
<h3>Step four: drink carefully</h3>
Hold F to drink. One sip in the footage was 0.5 L, so a 2 L bottle is about four sips. Check the item panel for the words clean or untreated before you hold the key.
<h3>A ten minute checklist</h3>
<ul><li>Fill the bucket, carry it to the rack, then fill it again if the taps still run.</li><li>Open the mirror cabinet and take the tablets with you.</li><li>Fill every bottle and the jerrycan before moving on to food.</li><li>Do not let the objective timer rush you into leaving the tap on; turn it off when it says Full.</li></ul>
After water comes food: carry every item to the pantry and read the counter. Our <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/food-and-water">food and water page</a> has the counts seen so far. If you want the whole first week, the <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/day-by-day">day by day page</a> lists each objective, and the <a href="https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/guide">30 floors up guide</a> has a tick-off checklist. Another note on common first-night errors is here: <a href="https://robloxguidesdaily.blogspot.com/2026/11/30-floors-up-7-beginner-mistakes.html">7 beginner mistakes</a>.
The numbers above come from a handful of public videos, and apartments differ, so read your own counters. We re-check the page when a new video shows something different.
