# 30 Floors Up Update 2.0 roof projects: shelter, garden, kite

<p>The Roblox title of <a href="https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up">30 floors up roblox</a> now starts with [UPDATE 2.0], and the game description promises rooftop shelters, escape plans and new routes. Nobody has published patch notes that the site could find, so everything here comes from player footage. Think of it as field notes on what the roof asks of you.</p>
<h3>What changed and the shelter</h3>
<p>The developer's event list shows Update 1.0 from Nov 3 to 5 and Update 2.0 from Nov 8 to 10, each with a one-line note. The game was last updated on Nov 10, 2026. Code sites list one code for the update, Update2. The <a href="https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/update-2">30 floors up update 2.0</a> page keeps the list of what is confirmed, and the 30 floors up updates page is the running log. Carry boards up to the roof and set them on the glowing outlines. The back brace needs a board and nails, and the legs need a hammer. When it is finished the game says the rooftop anchor stands. A Cold meter fills while you are on the roof, and the shelter is described as tied down, out of the wind, warm enough to wait out a night. Materials seen: boards, a Box of Nails and a Wire Mesh Roll. The full step list is on the <a href="https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/rooftop-shelter">30 floors up rooftop shelter</a> page.</p>
<h3>The garden</h3>
<p>There are wooden planters on the roof. Plant seed packets, then water each bed with 1 L by holding F. The prompt tracks the seedlings, for example day 2 of 6, and warns about dry soil. A Rain Barrel with a Funnel holds 20 L of untreated rain water. The 30 floors up rooftop garden page lists the numbers.</p>
<h3>The kite and the radio</h3>
<p>After the anchor stands, the game tells you to coil the static line on the wall, tie the kite to it and throw it from the mark. The mark is painted on the roof and faces the hospital. A Kite and String lay on a floor on Day 11 and a 40 m Static Line was on the roof on Day 12. Nobody has shown the throw yet, so the <a href="https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/kite-and-static-line">30 floors up kite and static line</a> page says it does not know what the line is for. Tune the battery radio to the City Emergency band (press I) and a recorded message tells residents able to travel to go to the stadium reception, open at the north and east gates. It says the stadium has its own generators, water and a secured perimeter, and that medical screening happens on arrival. The message was recorded at 06:00 on Day Two and repeats. The <a href="https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/stadium-broadcast">30 floors up stadium broadcast</a> page has all ten lines.</p>
<h3>Is there an ending?</h3>
<p>No finished escape has been shown in the footage the site checked. The lobby has a Successful Evacuations board next to Total Days Survived, and a Day 28 objective sends one run up through lift 2's shaft to the roof. See the <a href="https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/roof-and-escape">30 floors up roof and escape</a> page for what is confirmed and what is not.</p>
