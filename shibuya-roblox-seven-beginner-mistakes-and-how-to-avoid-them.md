# Shibuya Roblox: seven beginner mistakes and how to avoid them

<p><a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki">Shibuya Roblox</a> is a Jujutsu Kaisen inspired action game in beta, with 25 players per server. A lot of early frustration comes from the same handful of mistakes, so here they are in the order people tend to hit them, with the wiki page that explains each one. Everything is marked reported or confirmed on the pages themselves, and I have kept that distinction.</p>
<h3>1 and 2: codes and character slots</h3>
<p>Project Mugetsu, often shortened to PM, is a different game with its own codes, and its lists do not work in Shibuya. Even inside Shibuya the lists disagree. PUBLICTESTING and RANKEDFIXES are the two codes that at least two sources agree on, while SORRYFORLAG, BALANCEPATCH1, 2MVISITS, 7KLIKES and 8KLIKES are disputed. 2MVISITS in particular belongs to Shinobi Reborn. The <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/codes">Shibuya codes</a> page keeps the comparison. The Redeem Code box is in the top right of the Customize screen, and the rerolls go to the character you are customizing. Open the right slot before you type anything. The longer walk-through is on how to redeem Shibuya codes.</p>
<h3>3 and 4: rerolls and parrying</h3>
<p>Spend your rerolls before you lock the character in. The creation screen shows the odds: Death Painting 2%, Mutated Corpse 4%, Incarnated Sorcerer 7%, and Mythic clans 0.25% for the tier. The <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/reroll-odds-calculator">Shibuya reroll odds calculator</a> turns that into numbers, for example about 920 rerolls for a 90% chance at a Mythic clan. Holding F is a plain block. Tapping it right before a hit is the parry. M1 attacks, Q dashes, E infuses attacks with cursed energy and X launches an uppercut. The Shibuya controls page copies the key list from the Roblox description, so it is easy to check.</p>
<h3>5 and 6: containment and yen</h3>
<p>Containment is a separate step after the kill. Weaken the curse, hold E on the contain prompt until it says contained, then hand it in to Agent Hoshino inside the HQ building. The HQ Lab pays Merit, and Black Net pays Sin. The <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/curse-containment">Shibuya curse containment</a> guide has the full loop. The route to a cursed technique needs Grade 2, a 5,000 yen gas mask from Tomoe, a 10,000 yen gate fee for Jira and a bicycle rental whose price nobody has written down. Techniques cost 12,000 to 40,000 yen. Keep 15,000 yen in reserve for the mask and the gate. The <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/cursed-techniques">Shibuya cursed techniques</a> page lays out the whole route.</p>
<h3>7. Assuming Mythic means better</h3>
<p>Rarity is not the same as value. The wiki marks most clan bonuses as player reported, and only Hakari's +75 HP and Gojo's 3% Six Eyes awaken chance have a number attached, both from one fan site. Pick by bonus, not by label. See <a href="https://shibuyawiki.wiki/races-and-clans">Shibuya races and clans</a>.</p>
