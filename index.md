# 30 Floors Up Roblox guide: Night 0 checklist and Day 1 rations

30 Floors Up is a Roblox survival game set in an apartment block where a city-wide disturbance traps everyone indoors. The first evening decides how comfortable your first days are, so here is the short version of what to do before the lights go out. The full step-by-step version lives in the [30 Floors Up guide](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/guide), and the main [30 Floors Up Roblox wiki](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up) keeps classes, weapons, floors and live player counts in one place.

## Night 0 in four steps

1. **Look around.** Walk the rooms while the TV is still on. A breaking-news cut-in tells residents to stay inside, and at 19:30 the signal drops.
2. **Store water while the taps run.** Take the cleaning bucket from under the kitchen sink, fill it in the bathtub, and carry it to the metal water rack in the living room.
3. **Collect all the food.** Fridge, cupboards and the shopping bag all go to the pantry shelves. Tinned food needs a can opener before you can eat it.
4. **Lock up and answer the door.** Secure the apartment, then let Daniel in when he knocks.

## Day 1: plan your rations

Check the counter that shows how many portions you have stored and decide how many you can spend per day. The bars on screen (hunger, thirst and the rest) drop faster than you expect, so food and water management is the real game. If you want to know what you can buy, the [30 Floors Up game passes](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/game-passes) page lists them, and the [30 Floors Up codes](https://games.resuhive.com/30-floors-up/codes) page tracks whether any working codes exist right now.
