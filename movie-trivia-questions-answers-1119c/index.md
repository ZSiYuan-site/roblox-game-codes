# Movie Trivia Questions and Answers: Start With the Kids Set

Movie trivia is one of the easiest topics to enter because most people have seen the films. The Trivia Nook movie page was updated on November 19, 2026, and it holds 30 questions split into three sets of ten: for kids, for adults and hard. Everything is free and needs no sign-up, and each answer comes with a short explanation. Here is how the set is built and why the easiest level is still worth playing.

## Thirty questions in three bands

The [movie trivia questions and answers](https://trivia.resuhive.com/movies/) page opens with a ten-question online quiz, then prints all three sets below it. The kids set uses animated favourites and family films. The adults set moves to classic cinema, famous lines and directors. The hard set is for people who already know the standard answers. You can play online with instant feedback and no timer, or print the page and use it as a quiz sheet.

## The kids set is not filler

The easy questions are specific enough to teach something. One asks which Disney film features Let It Go; the answer is Frozen, and the explanation notes that the song won the Oscar for Best Original Song. Another asks for the cowboy doll in Toy Story, which is Woody, voiced by Tom Hanks. Simba's father is Mufasa, voiced by James Earl Jones, and Nemo is a clownfish while Dory is a blue tang. The Wizard of Oz question asks for Dorothy's dog, Toto, played by a Cairn terrier named Terry. Those are details you can check, which is the point.

## Stepping up to adults

The adults set starts with Wings, which won the first Academy Award for Outstanding Picture at the 1929 ceremony. Jaws was directed by Steven Spielberg and is often called the first summer blockbuster. Parasite won Best Picture at the 2020 Oscars, the first non-English-language winner, and also took Best Director and Best International Feature. Marlon Brando played Vito Corleone in The Godfather, and the line Here's looking at you, kid comes from Casablanca.

## A way to use the page

Play the kids set first, even if you think it is too easy; it warms up the reasoning. Then take the adults set without reading the answers printed below. Check your score against the explanations rather than just the letters. The site has ten topics in total, and [general knowledge trivia questions and answers](https://trivia.resuhive.com/general-knowledge/) is a good next stop once the movie set feels comfortable.
