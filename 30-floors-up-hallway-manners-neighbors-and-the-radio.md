# 30 Floors Up hallway manners: who knocks, who you trade with and what the radio costs

Most 30 Floors Up advice is about buckets and doors, but the game also puts a handful of talkable neighbors in your hallway, and the Roblox description tells you to help them. This guide treats them like people you share a corridor with: who they are, what they ask for and what each favor costs you in noise. Everything here is from player footage and the game's own text; we say so where nobody has shown the rest. The [30 floors up guide](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/guide) has the first-night order, and the [neighbors page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/neighbors) has the full list with lines.

## Rule one: peephole first

The door gives you three actions: unlock with F, peephole with R and the door chain with T. The Night 0 objective, Lock up for the night, asks for the peephole before you answer. That habit applies to every neighbor below.

## Daniel, Night 0

At 21:30 someone knocks and a timer shows how long they will wait. You check, unlock, let Daniel in and close the door. His advice is to keep the noise down, and they stay put. The [let Daniel in page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/let-daniel-in) covers the steps.

## Ruth and Sam Okafor

Ruth warns you off the stairs in the hallway: the west stairwell is full of them. Sam is a neighbor you can talk to with E near the Okafors' door. A locked door nearby reads as their private rooms with nothing you need, and the Small Brass Key you may find near it opens them, so do not waste time there.

## Mara and the radio

Mara is across the balcony gap. The objective Food for Mara's radio asks you to hand her 2 portions across the gap, and she says thank you. The next objective reads: switch the radio on at low volume shortly before 19:00 and close the doors, because loud radios draw them in. She also says someone called Ward comes on at seven on the relay band.

- F switches the radio on or off.
- I tunes to the next band, North Relay or City Emergency.
- Holding T fits fresh batteries; the card shows the battery percentage.
- Keep it low: the game description for the Custom Music Radio pass says the infected hear any battery radio.

See the [30 floors up radio page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/radio) and the [stadium broadcast page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/stadium-broadcast) for the City Emergency message.

## Arthur Hale and Eli

Arthur wears a hi-vis vest and knows about the roof key. On Day 20 an objective sends you down lift 2's shaft to floor 28 to get him out of the maintenance room, with a hammer or screwdriver and a bandage; he has no water left after Day 21. Eli is hiding. He asks whether the hallway is quiet, and when you say it is clear, he begs you not to leave him.

## What helping costs

Every favor has a price in either supplies or noise. Mara costs two portions. Arthur costs a trip and a bandage. Eli costs you time and a moment of silence. In one co-op run, the group gave a neighbor food and got a radio back, so at least some help comes back. We have not seen how far trading goes.
