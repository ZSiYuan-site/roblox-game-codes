# 30 Floors Up plumbing days: the latched toolbox, the riser valve and two keys

Between Day 2 and Day 6, 30 Floors Up quietly sets up a small chain of jobs: a toolbox that will not open, a wrench inside it and a seized valve that feeds the taps. Players who skip the chain run out of water earlier than they need to. Here it is in order, from the footage we have read. If you want the broader survival route, start with the [30 floors up day by day](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/day-by-day) page.

## Step 1: the latched toolbox (Day 2)

The Day 2 objective is to fill every container before the taps stop, but the same day a red toolbox appears with the prompt Inside the latched toolbox. In the footage the player holds a Maintenance Key Ring at 02:50, then a red Emergency Cupboard Key later that night, and the message says the latch clicks open: pliers, a wrench and a tag inside. We could not see which key did it, because there is no lock animation. The [maintenance key ring page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/maintenance-key-ring) explains the two KEY cards.

## Step 2: pick up the Adjustable Wrench

It is tagged as a tool, with Damage 16 and Reach 4.5, so it is a poor weapon next to the Baseball Bat at 26 and 8.0. Keep the bat in another slot. The [adjustable wrench page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/adjustable-wrench) lists where it was seen, including an Undo the bolt prompt on a floor plate on Day 3.

## Step 3: the riser valve (Day 5 to Day 6)

On Day 5 the hint reads: a seized red wheel valve on the risers, it needs a wrench. The valve is a red wheel on two blue pipes with a pressure gauge. Turn it with the wrench in hand and by Day 6 the message is that the riser valve is open and the north tank feeds the taps upstairs. The [riser valve page](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/riser-valve) has the prompts.

## Step 4: put out buckets for the rain

Day 6 is also the day clouds build. The objective says open containers on the balcony brackets will collect rain. That water is untreated, so keep your tablets close and read the [treat water in 30 floors up](https://newgamescout.com/30-floors-up/treat-water) steps before you drink any.

## The cupboard you cannot open yet

A steel-faced cupboard with a first-aid cross is locked in the same footage. The game offers Force the door, loud, by holding G with the hammer. We never saw the cupboard key used on it, so we do not list what is inside. Forcing a door is loud, so clear the corridor first.

## Why it matters later

The wrench keeps being useful: a bent valve handle on the roof wants one, the Day 28 machine room hatch wants one, and Day 31 to 32 sends you to floor 27 to open the north tank's valve with one again.
