# Blox Fruits Value List and Tier List: What the S to D Tiers Mean


A tier list is the fastest way to remember which fruits matter. This post walks through the tier list on [Blox Fruits Values](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/tier-list) and explains how the tiers are built, so you can use them next to the [blox fruits value list](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/values) when you plan a trade.

## How the tiers are defined

Items are sorted into five tiers by Beli price, using the Normal version of each item. The S tier starts at $8M and above. 74 items have a value, and the 13 without one are not ranked. There is no hidden score and no vote. If an item moves tiers, it is because its shop price in the Blox Fruits Wiki changed.

## The tiers as listed

- S tier ($8M and up, 3 items): Dragon Fruit at $15M, Control Fruit at $9M, Kitsune Fruit at $8M.
- A tier ($3.2M to $8M, 7 items): Magnet Fruit at $6M, Godhuman, Sanguine Art, Tiger Fruit and Yeti Fruit at $5M each, Spirit Fruit at $3.4M and Gas Fruit at $3.2M.
- B tier ($1M to $3.2M, 27 items): the biggest group, including Dragon Talon, Electric Claw, Superhuman, Venom Fruit, Shadow Fruit, Dough Fruit, Mammoth Fruit and T-Rex Fruit.

The remaining items fall into the lower tiers, and you can see every one of them on the page itself. Notice that the B tier holds more items than all the tiers above it combined, which tells you the market is wide in the middle and narrow at the top.

## Why Beli price is the sorting key

Blox Fruits has no official trade value, so the site starts with the one number the game publishes. The [how values work](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/how-values-work) page gives the example of Dragon Fruit at $15,000,000 Beli, and Magma Fruit at $960,000 Beli for the middle of the list. This makes tiers verifiable: you can open the shop and see the same prices. It also means tiers describe cost, not demand.

## Using the tier list while trading

Here is a practical routine. First, find the tier of each item in the offer. Trading within the same tier is usually close to even by price, while trading across two tiers deserves a closer look. Second, check the exact numbers on the list, because the gap between the top and the bottom of a tier can be large. The B tier, for example, runs from $1M to $3.2M. Third, put both sides into the calculator to get a Win, Fair or Lose label.

The [blox fruits trade calculator](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/) on the home page does step three for you, and it explains its bands: Fair means less than 5 percent apart, and a big Win or Lose means the sides are more than 49 percent apart.

## Keep an eye on the changes

Prices can change when the wiki updates. The site keeps a Movers page that lists the biggest value changes between two syncs, and an updates log. At the time of writing the last sync was November 15, 2026, and the Movers page showed no rising, falling or new items since November 11. Check [the Movers page](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/movers) before a big trade, because a tier list that is a week old can be wrong about a fruit that just changed.

One more limit to keep in mind: the lists do not include demand. A fruit in a lower tier can still be what everyone wants this week. Use the tiers to organize your thinking, and let the market tell you the rest.
