# Pips Hard Boards Have Grown: From 10 Dominoes in August 2025 to 16 in November 2026

Anyone who has played NYT Pips since the start has probably noticed that the Hard board has gotten bigger. The archive lets us check that. The [Pips answers archive](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/archive/) lists 458 puzzles, and the answer pages show how many dominoes each board uses.

## The first Hard board

The earliest puzzle in the archive is August 18, 2025. On that day the [Pips answer for August 18, 2025](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/answers/2025-08-18/) had Easy (4 dominoes), Medium (5) and Hard (10). The Hard board that day was built by Heidi Erwin and had 1 less-than, 3 equal and 5 sum regions.

## Samples along the way

Here are the three board sizes for a few dates, written Easy / Medium / Hard:

- Aug 18, 2025: 4 / 5 / 10
- Sep 15, 2025: 4 / 8 / 12
- Oct 13, 2025: 6 / 6 / 7
- Nov 17, 2025: 5 / 8 / 13
- Dec 25, 2025: 5 / 9 / 15
- Jan 1, 2026: 4 / 7 / 12
- Apr 20, 2026: 6 / 7 / 16
- Nov 16, 2026: 5 / 7 / 16

## What the numbers say

The Easy board has hovered between 4 and 6 dominoes the whole time. Medium moves between 5 and 9 but has been 7 most days lately. Hard is where the change is. It started at 10, dipped to 7 on a day in October 2025, and then settled in the 12 to 16 range. The newest boards, such as [November 16, 2026](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/answers/2026-11-16/), use 16.

## The odd day

October 13, 2025 is worth a look because its Hard board has only 7 dominoes and 3 equal regions, which is smaller than its Easy board of 6 plus a Medium of 6. It is a reminder that the labels do not guarantee order of size. A Hard puzzle with big equal regions can fall faster than a Medium one with many sums.

## How to use the pattern

1. If today's Hard board has 16 dominoes, plan more time. Start with every one-cell region before anything else.
2. If it has fewer, look for large equal regions. They are the fastest way in.
3. Use Hint 1 on the answer page. It tells you the number of dominoes and the mix of region types before you look at any answer.

## About the data

Each answer page names who built each board. Across the samples above, Ian Livengood, Rodolfo Kurchan and Heidi Erwin appear as constructors, and the page says the day's puzzle was edited by Ian Livengood. The boards come from the game's own data, which is why they match what you see in the app.
